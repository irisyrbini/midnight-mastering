# Story & tone

> Source of truth for narrative intent and voice. Content is currently environmental and systemic
> rather than scripted; this doc governs any authored text added later. It is downstream of
> [Vision.md](Vision.md) — read that first. **Patch 0.2:** this game is about *unfinished dreams and
> permission*, not depression or healing; the voice reflects that.

## 1. Premise

*Make Me Happy Again* follows one music producer, alone in a small apartment studio, awake after
everyone else has gone home, trying to **finish something they started a long time ago.** The album is
real work, but it is really a stand-in for every unfinished dream a person quietly carries. The game is
the small, patient act of **giving yourself permission to finish one chapter** — not a struggle to be
fixed, but a night spent returning to the work and to yourself.

## 2. Theme

**Unfinished dreams, and the permission to continue.** Creative work grows slowly; the game says that
is fine. It is not about talent or triumph — it is about persistence, presence, and letting *finishing
something* be enough. Rest, food, contact, and looking out the window are not distractions from the
work; they are part of how the work gets finished.

## 3. Emotional arc

Not "sad → happy." The producer moves through:

**Uncertain → Present → Comfortable → Hopeful → Ready to continue.**

The album can only be completed with a **green crystal**, which asks for hope, love, and reconnection.
Story and mechanics say the same thing: you finish the record not by grinding but by becoming present
enough to want to. The end state the player should carry out of the game is *ready to continue* —
ideally ready to close the game and return to their own real, unfinished work.

## 4. Voice & register

- Second-person-adjacent, quiet, specific. Interaction lines are short and tender ("Play something only
  you need to hear.").
- **Whisper, not shout.** No motivational or self-help language, no "You can do it!", never telling the
  player how to feel. Write as if **the world understands them** — reassurance is environmental, not
  instructional.
- Never clinical, never melodramatic. No diagnosis language. No jokes at the character's expense.
- Coping objects are written without judgment but with honesty about cost ("Borrow energy from
  tomorrow.").

## 5. Dialogue principles

NPCs (the visiting friends) should **rarely give advice.** They **share experience** — a memory, a small
admission, something they once made or failed to finish. The player should feel **understood, not
instructed.** No pep talks, no life lessons, no "have you tried…".

## 6. Representation guardrails (binding)

- Emotional and creative states are shown through light, rhythm, density, and sound-adjacent metaphor —
  **never** monsters, body horror, self-harm imagery, or "madness" tropes (see [ArtBible.md](ArtBible.md)
  §1, §7).
- Broaden any older "mental-health" framing to the creative audience in [Vision.md](Vision.md) (makers of
  every kind, not a clinical target group).
- No brands, no real people, no copyrighted lyrics or album art.
- An unfinished night is quiet. It is framed as *the night ending*, never spectacle or punishment.

## 7. Ending framing

Endings are **completion, not victory.** Keep them quiet.

- **Finished:** the record is done and the producer is present enough to know it. Aim for the structure:
  the music finishes → morning arrives → silence → then *"The work is finished."* (pause) *"But your
  story isn't."* Release, not triumph; the next chapter is another opportunity to create, not a sequel.
- **Unfinished night (the "collapse" state in code):** the producer runs out of tonight and can't
  continue. Gentle, non-punishing; the restart is an invitation to come back, never a game-over sting.

## 8. Environmental storytelling

The room should become **evidence that a life has been lived — an archive of creation**, and the
player's **silent companion** that grows subtly warmer as work progresses (morning light reaching
farther, plants growing, objects accumulating, birds outside, ambient sound shifting). Prefer leaving
**subtle memories** over any XP/reward pop-up: coffee stains, sticky notes, used sketchbooks, old train
tickets, polaroids, cassette tapes, guitar picks, notes in notebook margins. Nothing should loudly
congratulate — the world just quietly fills in around the persistence.

## 9. Future narrative (planned)

Authored beats keyed to `day` and crystal state, delivered through the existing interaction-log / video
surfaces and stored in `src/data`. Any such content must pass the guardrails above and answer the one
question in [Vision.md](Vision.md): *does this bring the player closer to creative peace?*
